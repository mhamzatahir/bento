WELCOME BEST PLAYERS TIPS

Tips

Below are few tips to improve your game of Chess:

1. Control the center:

Put your pawns and pieces on or close to the central four squares (d4, e4, d5, e5). The pieces in the center control more squares.

2. Develop your pieces early:

Develop your knights and bishops before repeating moves. Move knights first (towards the center), then bishops and do not develop your queen too early since she gets chased around.

3. Castle early

Castle protects your king with pawns and places the rook in play. Clear the squares between your king and rook, then castle within your first ten moves.

4. Check for threats before every move

Beginners' games are usually lost because of mistakes rather than strategy. Before moving, ask yourself, "What is my opponent threatening?" and "Is my piece safe?"

5. Do not give away material for free

Always count material in your exchanges. Pawn = 1, knight/bishop = 3, rook = 5, queen = 9. Exchange pieces only when you gain equal or more material than the one you exchange.
